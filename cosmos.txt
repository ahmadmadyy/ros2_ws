### 1. Phase Segmentation
Break the trajectory into semantic phases matching the pick-then-screw workflow. Expected phases include:

**Pick phases:**
- **Home**: robot moves to home joint configuration
- **Open gripper**: gripper opens fully before approach
- **Approach screwdriver**: arm moves above the screwdriver (top-down) at approach height
- **Descend to grasp**: arm lowers to grasp height with tool0 directly above screwdriver
- **Close gripper**: gripper closes to the target opening that fits the screwdriver shaft
- **Retreat upward**: arm lifts vertically with screwdriver grasped

**Screw phases:**
- **Move above screw target**: arm repositions laterally to hover above the screw location
- **Lower to engage screw**: arm descends so the screwdriver tip contacts the screw head
- **CW wrist rotation (screwing stroke)**: wrist_3 rotates clockwise in incremental steps from its current value toward the lower safe limit, driving the screw
- **Lift to disengage**: arm lifts to approach height while retaining the current joint configuration (except height)
- **CCW wrist reposition**: wrist_3 rotates counter-clockwise back to the upper safe limit while the screwdriver is lifted clear of the screw (no screw driving occurs)
- **Lower to re-engage**: arm descends again to re-seat the screwdriver on the screw head for the next stroke
- *(CW → lift → CCW → lower repeats for each cycle)*

**Return phases:**
- **Lift off after final cycle**: arm lifts away from screw after last CW stroke
- **Return home**: arm moves to home joint configuration

For each phase, state the approximate timestep range and what the robot was doing. For wrist rotation phases, note the start and end wrist_3 values and the total angle traversed.
