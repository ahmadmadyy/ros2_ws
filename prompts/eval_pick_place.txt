## Evaluation Rubric

Score each dimension from 1 (worst) to 5 (best). Provide a brief justification for each.

### A. Reasoning Quality
1. **Spatial Awareness** (1-5): Does the reasoning correctly identify object positions, obstacles, and spatial relationships?
2. **Plan Coherence** (1-5): Are the proposed steps logically ordered and physically feasible? Are there missing steps or contradictions?
3. **Safety Awareness** (1-5): Does the reasoning account for collision avoidance, joint limits, and gripper constraints?

### B. Trajectory Quality
4. **Goal Achievement** (1-5): Based on the final joint configuration, does the robot appear to reach the target placement? (5 = final pose is consistent with goal position, 1 = clearly far off)
5. **Efficiency** (1-5): Is the trajectory reasonably direct, or are there unnecessary detours, reversals, or excessive waypoints?
6. **Smoothness** (1-5): Are joint angle transitions gradual and physically realistic, or are there sudden large jumps that suggest jerky or unsafe motion?

### C. Reasoning-Trajectory Alignment
7. **Consistency** (1-5): Does the actual trajectory follow the plan described in the reasoning? If the reasoning says "approach from above," does the trajectory reflect that?
