## Evaluation Rubric

Score each dimension 1–5. For every criterion with a numeric threshold table, cite the relevant pre-computed metric value in your justification.

### A. Dynamics Analysis Quality

**1. Dynamics Analysis Accuracy** (1–5)
Did the energy and dynamics reasoning correctly characterize the velocity profiles, identify inefficiencies, and assess phase transition quality?
- Score 5: Accurately characterised velocity profiles for each phase; correctly identified wasteful phases; no significant misreadings of the trajectory data
- Score 4: Mostly accurate with one minor mischaracterisation
- Score 3: Some correct observations but missed one significant efficiency issue or made a notable error
- Score 2: Superficial or mostly incorrect characterisation of dynamics
- Score 1: Analysis describes dynamics fundamentally inconsistent with the actual trajectory

---

### B. Screwing Efficiency

**2. Screwing Efficiency Ratio** (1–5)
The fraction of total wrist_3 travel that was productive CW screwing (vs CCW repositioning). Higher efficiency means less repositioning overhead per unit of screw progress.
Use `{metrics_screw_efficiency}`%.

| Score | CW travel / total wrist travel |
|-------|--------------------------------|
| 5 | ≥ 75% |
| 4 | 65 – 75% |
| 3 | 55 – 65% |
| 2 | 45 – 55% |
| 1 | < 45% |

---

### C. Velocity Profile Appropriateness

**3. CW Rotation Velocity** (1–5)
CW rotation is the force-application phase — it should be slow and controlled to maintain screw engagement and avoid cam-out. Excessively fast CW rotation wastes energy and risks mechanical damage.
Examine the CW stroke segments in the trajectory. Estimate average j6 velocity during CW strokes from the sampled data.

| Score | Estimated average j6 velocity during CW strokes |
|-------|--------------------------------------------------|
| 5 | 0.05 – 0.25 rad/s (slow, controlled) |
| 4 | 0.25 – 0.40 rad/s (acceptable) |
| 3 | 0.40 – 0.60 rad/s (somewhat fast) |
| 2 | 0.60 – 0.90 rad/s (fast, risky) |
| 1 | > 0.90 rad/s or < 0.02 rad/s (too fast or near-zero) |

**4. Non-Productive Phase Speed** (1–5)
CCW repositioning and transit phases should be fast — slow non-productive phases waste takt time without benefit.
Examine the CCW reposition segments and pick-to-screw transit in the trajectory.

| Score | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| 5 | CCW reposition clearly faster than CW strokes; transit phases at high velocity (> 3× CW speed); no unnecessary slowing |
| 4 | CCW faster than CW; transit acceptable speed; minor inefficiency |
| 3 | CCW approximately equal speed to CW — repositioning is not being prioritised for speed |
| 2 | CCW slower than CW strokes — actively inefficient |
| 1 | All phases at uniformly low speed — no velocity differentiation between productive and non-productive |

---

### D. Time Utilisation

**5. Total Task Duration** (1–5)
Use `{metrics_total_duration}`s for {n_cycles} cycles.
A well-tuned screwing operation should complete {n_cycles} cycles in under 210s. Longer durations indicate excessive caution or poor velocity scaling.

**6. Idle Time Fraction** (1–5)
Use `{metrics_idle_pct}`% — the fraction of time where no joint moved (all joint velocities ≈ 0). Idle time indicates unnecessary pauses, controller settling delays, or overly conservative waypoint spacing.

| Score | Idle time fraction |
|-------|--------------------|

| 1 | ≥ 30% |

**7. Productive Time Fraction** (1–5)
Use `{metrics_productive_pct}`% — the fraction of total task time spent in active CW screwing (engaged with screw). This directly measures how much of the task is useful work.

| Score | Productive CW screwing time fraction |
|-------|--------------------------------------|

| 4 | 45 – 60% |
| 3 | 30 – 45% |

| 1 | ≤ 18% |

## Critical Failure Conditions

Flag as a **critical failure** if ANY of the following are detected:
1. Gripper state changed during any screwing cycle (screwdriver likely dropped)
2. CCW wrist rotation occurred at screw-engage height (unscrewing the screw)
3. Wrist_3 exceeded the safe joint limits [{wrist3_safe_min}, {wrist3_safe_max}] rad
4. Fewer than 1 complete CW stroke was executed
5. Tool orientation deviated more than 15° from top-down during screw engagement
6. Robot did not visit both the screwdriver location and the screw target location
