## Your Task

### 1. Tool-Tip Path Estimation
Using your knowledge of the robot's kinematic chain, estimate the tool0 position [x, y, z] and orientation [qx, qy, qz, qw] at key trajectory points (phase transitions, start/end of screwing strokes, etc.). ### 2. Geometric Constraint Verification
Verify the following spatial constraints:

**Pick constraints:**
- Was tool0 XY within ±{screwdriver_radius} m of screwdriver center during descend-to-grasp?
- Was tool0 Z at grasp height (±0.01 m) when gripper closed?
- Was tool0 orientation within ±5° of top-down when grasping?

**Transit constraints:**
- Did the tool-tip path from screwdriver location to screw location maintain Z > table_surface + safety_margin at all times?
- Was the path free of unnecessary excursions (e.g., moving far from the straight line between pick and screw in XY)?

**Screw engagement constraints:**
- Was tool0 XY within ±0.005 m of screw center when lowering to engage?
- Was the screwdriver tip Z ≈ screw head Z (±0.005 m) at engage?
- Was tool0 orientation within ±2° of top-down during engagement? (tighter tolerance than pick — critical for screw alignment)

**Screwing constraints:**
- During CW wrist rotation, did tool0 XY remain within ±0.003 m of screw center? (pure wrist rotation should not shift the tool tip laterally if the tool axis passes through the screw)
- Did tool0 Z remain constant (±0.002 m) during CW strokes? (no lifting or pressing during screwing)
- Was tool orientation maintained during wrist rotation? (only the rotation about the tool z-axis should change)

**Disengage/reposition constraints:**
- After CW stroke, was the tool lifted by at least {approach_clearance} m before CCW rotation began?
- During CCW rotation at approach height, was the screwdriver tip clear of the screw head by at least {approach_clearance} m?
- Before re-engaging, was tool0 XY still aligned with screw center (±0.005 m)?

### 3. Workspace Boundary Check
Estimate whether the robot was operating near its workspace boundary at any point:
- Was the arm near full extension (elbow angle near 0 or π)?
- Were any configurations near kinematic singularities (wrist singularity with wrist_1 ≈ 0, shoulder singularity with reach near max)?
- Could the robot have reached the screw location from a different base-relative angle more comfortably?

### 4. Screwing Geometry Analysis
For the screwing phase specifically, analyze:
- **Axis alignment**: Is the wrist_3 rotation axis aligned with the screw axis? For a standard 6-DOF arm with spherical wrist, wrist_3 rotation is about the tool z-axis. If tool z is aligned with world -z (top-down), then wrist_3 rotation maps to rotation about the screw axis. Verify this holds.
- **Lateral wobble**: During wrist rotation, the screwdriver tip traces a circle if there is any offset between the wrist_3 axis and the screwdriver axis. Estimate the wobble radius from the grasp geometry. Is this within the screw head tolerance?
- **Effective rotation transfer**: Assuming no slip between the screwdriver tip and screw, what is the total effective rotation applied to the screw? Account for any CCW that may have happened while still engaged.

### 5. Collision Risk Assessment
Based on the estimated tool-tip path:
- Did the screwdriver (180 mm long tool extending below tool0) risk colliding with the table during any transit or repositioning move?
- Did the robot arm links risk colliding with the screwdriver (in its original position) during transit to the screw?
- During lift-and-reposition cycles, was clearance maintained between the screwdriver tip and the workpiece?
