### A. Fault Analysis Quality

**1. Fault Analysis Accuracy** (1–5)
Did the fault-detection reasoning correctly identify the actual anomalies present in this trajectory?
- Score 5: All significant anomalies correctly flagged with accurate timestamps and magnitudes; no false positives; correctly identified clean phases as clean
- Score 4: Most anomalies correctly identified; minor false positive or one missed issue
- Score 3: Some correct findings but missed one significant anomaly or made a notable false positive
- Score 2: Superficial analysis; missed multiple significant anomalies or majority of findings were incorrect
- Score 1: Analysis was inaccurate or described a trajectory fundamentally different from the actual data

---

## Critical Failure Conditions

Flag as a **critical failure** if ANY of the following are detected:
1. Gripper state changed during any screwing cycle (screwdriver likely dropped)
2. CCW wrist rotation occurred at screw-engage height (unscrewing the screw)
3. Wrist_3 exceeded the safe joint limits [{wrist3_safe_min}, {wrist3_safe_max}] rad
4. Fewer than 1 complete CW stroke was executed
5. Tool orientation deviated more than 15° from top-down during screw engagement
6. Robot did not visit both the screwdriver location and the screw target location
