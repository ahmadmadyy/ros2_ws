**SECTION 5 — STRATEGY ASSESSMENT**

The “continuous screw with periodic wrist reset” strategy works by enabling sustained CW rotation via the wrist_3_joint while periodically unwinding CCW to reset the spiral. This approach sacrifices some precision for simplicity and throughput, especially in a top-down approach where a single screw may span multiple cycles. Tradeoffs include reduced repeatability compared to a single uninterrupted spiral and increased risk of tool misalignment during resets. Alternatives like regripping the tool mid-task or employing a different tool orientation could improve accuracy but require more complex control logic. For a top-down approach, this strategy is acceptable but warrants refinement in reset timing and spiral smoothing.
