Robotic Screwing Task — Detailed Explanation
Robot: UR5e 6-DOF manipulator with Robotiq 85 parallel gripper
Script: tests/test_screw_topdown.py


--- Overview ---

The screwing task is a 13-step sequence that starts from a neutral home
position, picks up a screwdriver from the table using a top-down grasp,
carries it to a screw target, drives the screw in with three full
clockwise rotations, and then returns home. The whole sequence is
planned and executed through MoveIt, with the gripper controlled
separately via the Robotiq driver.

The screwdriver is a 24 mm diameter, 180 mm tall cylinder standing
upright at x=0.45, y=0.00 on the table. The screw target is at
x=0.40, y=0.10, flush with the table surface (z=0.0). The grasp
happens at the midpoint of the shaft (z=0.09), which puts the tool0
frame at z=0.26 when the fingers are properly positioned.


--- The 13 Steps ---

Step 1 — Home position

The arm is moved to the canonical home configuration:
[0, -90°, 0°, -90°, 0°, 0°]. This is the starting point for every
run and also where the sequence ends. It places the arm in a safe,
upright posture with no risk of self-collision or table collision.


Step 2 — Open gripper

The gripper is fully opened before any approach. This is done first so
there is no chance of accidentally nudging the screwdriver during the
descent. A closed gripper approaching a narrow cylinder would likely
knock it over.


Step 3 — Approach screwdriver (top-down)

The arm moves to a position directly above the screwdriver, 18 cm
above the grasp point (tool0 at z=0.44). The orientation throughout
the pick sequence is top-down: a 180° rotation about the world X axis,
which points tool0 straight down. An IK seed is used here to force the
solver into the "arm forward" configuration and keep the solution
consistent with the subsequent descent step.


Step 4 — Allow gripper/screwdriver collision

Before descending, the collision between the gripper links and the
screwdriver object is explicitly allowed in the MoveIt planning scene.
Without this, the collision checker would reject any plan that brings
the fingers close to the cylinder, making the grasp impossible to plan.
This is a deliberate scene configuration step, not a physical action.


Step 5 — Descend to grasp

The arm lowers straight down to the grasp pose (tool0 at z=0.26), which
positions the finger tips at the midpoint of the screwdriver shaft
(z=0.09). The descent uses a slower velocity scaling (0.2 vs 0.3 for
most moves) to reduce the chance of overshooting or bumping the
screwdriver. The IK seed from the approach step is reused so the arm
stays in the same configuration branch throughout the descent.


Step 6 — Close gripper onto shaft

The gripper is commanded to close to 0.57 rad, which corresponds to
roughly a 24 mm gap between the finger tips — just wide enough to grip
the shaft without crushing it. This is calculated from the Robotiq 85
geometry: the maximum opening maps to 0 rad and the gripper scales
linearly, so a 24 mm gap on an 85 mm max-opening gripper gives
0.79 × (85-24)/85 ≈ 0.57 rad.


Step 7 — Attach screwdriver to tool0 in planning scene

After the physical grip is established, the screwdriver object in the
MoveIt planning scene is attached to the tool0 link. From this point
forward, the planner treats the screwdriver as part of the robot body,
so it will be included in collision checks and will move with the arm
in subsequent steps. The full set of gripper links is passed as
"touch links" so the planner does not flag the already-grasped object
as a collision.


Step 8 — Retreat upward

The arm lifts 20 cm above the grasp point (tool0 at z=0.46) to clear
the table and any surrounding objects before translating horizontally
to the screw location. Moving up first avoids any risk of dragging the
screwdriver tip across the table surface during the transit.


Step 9 — Move above screw target

The arm translates horizontally to a position directly above the screw,
with a 15 cm safety clearance above the engagement height (tool0 at
z=0.41). The orientation is kept identical to the grasp orientation
throughout so the screwdriver tip remains pointing straight down and
aligned with the screw axis.


Step 10 — Lower to engage screw

The arm descends to the engagement pose (tool0 at z=0.26), which brings
the screwdriver tip down to exactly z=0.0 — the table surface where the
screw sits. This step uses a reduced velocity scaling (0.15) because the
tip needs to seat precisely into the screw head without overshooting.
The IK result from this step is saved separately, as it serves as the
base joint configuration for the rotation steps that follow.


Step 11 — Rotate wrist_3 clockwise × 3 turns

This is the actual screwing motion. Starting from the engage joint
configuration captured in step 10, wrist_3 (joint index 5) is
decremented by π/2 radians per step, twelve steps in total (3 turns ×
4 steps per turn). Each step is planned and executed as a separate joint
move, with a 200 ms pause between steps to let the joint settle before
the next command is sent. Clockwise rotation when viewed from above
corresponds to decreasing wrist_3 values in the UR joint convention.
The wrist_3 joint has safe limits of ±5.983 rad, so three full turns
(−6.28 rad of total travel from the engage position) is checked against
those limits before any run.


Step 12 — Lift off screw

After the screwing motion is complete, the arm retreats back up to the
pre-screw approach height (tool0 at z=0.41). This disengages the
screwdriver tip from the screw head before any horizontal movement,
preventing the tip from dragging across the table on the way back.


Step 13 — Return home

The arm returns to the home joint configuration [0, -90°, 0°, -90°,
0°, 0°]. The screwdriver remains attached in the planning scene for
this move. At this point the sequence is complete and the result is
reported as either SUCCESS or FAILED depending on whether all steps
returned OK.


--- Key Geometry Summary ---

Screwdriver position:    x=0.45, y=0.00  (centre of base)
Screwdriver diameter:    24 mm (radius 12 mm)
Screwdriver height:      180 mm, standing upright
Grasp height (tool0):    z=0.26  (finger tips at shaft midpoint z=0.09)
Approach clearance:      18 cm above grasp → tool0 at z=0.44
Retreat clearance:       20 cm above grasp → tool0 at z=0.46

Screw position:          x=0.40, y=0.10, z=0.0 (flush with table)
Engage height (tool0):   z=0.26  (tip at z=0.0)
Pre-screw clearance:     15 cm above engage → tool0 at z=0.41

Gripper close position:  0.57 rad (~24 mm gap)
Screw turns:             3 CW, executed as 12 steps of 90° each
Wrist_3 step size:       -π/2 rad per step
Total wrist_3 travel:    -6.283 rad (3 full turns CW)
Wrist_3 safe range:      ±5.983 rad
